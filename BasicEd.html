Basic Education

The BVBC Basic Education Department utilizes the Accelerated Christian Education (A.C.E.) system, which began in the early 1970s through the efforts of dedicated parents who desired their children to be reared under the teachings of God's Word—the Bible. This vision became the heart and soul of the program, which has now reached over 130 countries, where parents desire to have their children's lives patterned after the Lord Jesus Christ. Your support is significant as we strive for excellence and continuous improvement for this school year and its succeeding years.

Nursery

Our youngest learners build strong foundations through the A.C.E. Learning-to-Read program, mastering basic skills in Math, English, Science, Social Studies, Word Building, and Filipino. By year-end, they earn a Certificate of Completion or receive guidance for remedial work if needed.

Elementary

In 6 years, students deepen their knowledge across core subjects and Philippine History. Completing the required PACEs qualifies them for graduation and promotion to Junior High. Students with diagnosed learning difficulties may follow a specialized track.

Junior High School

Covering Years 7-10, students follow either a General or Honors course. Completion of all academic requirements is essential for moving up to Senior High. Students may need to extend their stay if major requirements aren't met.
