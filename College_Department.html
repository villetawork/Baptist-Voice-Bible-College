BVBC College Programs

BVBC students are taught by qualified professors guided by biblical principles and directed by a CHED recognized Curriculum...

Bachelor in Theology

A degree focused on biblical studies, doctrine, church history, and ministry training. It prepares students for roles such as pastors, missionaries, and church leaders by deepening their knowledge of God's Word and practical ministry skills.

Bachelor of Science in Christian Education

A degree that equips future Christian educators to teach in schools or churches. It combines academic education principles with biblical values, preparing graduates to teach both general and faith-based subjects, often in preparation for the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET).
