Basic Education

▼

Qualification

For Christian School: (Basic Education)

  1. Students who are financially incapable to sustain financial obligation of the school. An ITR will be required to substantiate such claim.
  2. Student who are academically competent with a general average of 85% above.
  3. Students who have good moral conduct and are willing to be deepen their faith to Jesus Christ.
  4. Students who are able to pass the diagnostic test with remarkable score according to their grade level.
  5. Students who are able to pass the interview facilitated by the school Board of Trustees.


Application Process

BIBLE SCHOOL APPLICANTS:
  1. Interested applicants must inquire to the principal's office for preliminary interview.
  2. Fill-out scholarship application form with document attached such as birth certificate, school card, ITR, application letter and Brgy.
    Clearance.
  3. Applicant along with their parent are scheduled for interview by the Board of Trustees.
  4. For those senior students (2nd and up) who excel academically will have the special privilege to be a scholarship recipient but still need to
    provide necessary documents such as ITR, application letter, birth certificate and Brgy. clearance. Students who are qualified to the project
    are also subject for moral and spiritual review of the school administrator. Students under these criteria can also avail to cover their pending
    school fees on their latter curriculum year.
